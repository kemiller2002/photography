# Photography review and 2027 Indiana State Fair candidates

Reviewed October 2026. Working titles are descriptive, **not verified original filenames**. Judgments are subjective, based on chat images rather than full-resolution RAW files. Ratings are provisional, not predictions of judging results.

## Priority shortlist
1. **Suspended in the Woods** — environmental spider, 9–9.5/10. Strong use of scale, discovery and environmental bokeh. State Fair: strong yes.
2. **Architecture of Silk** — full geometric web, 9–9.5/10. Graphic structure, imperfect geometry, spider as focal punctuation. State Fair: strong yes.
3. **Face to Face** — head-on bee in orange flower, 9.25–9.5/10. Direct engagement and unusual framing. State Fair: yes.
4. **Night Watch** — flash-lit spider close-up, ~9/10. Excellent contrast and geometry. State Fair: possible.
5. **Garden Visitor** — side-profile dark bee on yellow marigold, 9–9.25/10. Strong silhouette and contact gesture; alternate to Face to Face.
6. **Orange Against Blue** — off-center marigold, ~9/10. Strong complementary colors; potential floral entry.
7. **Black Swallowtail** — first caterpillar, 8.5–9/10. Striking markings and upward diagonal; possible wildlife entry.
8. **The Last Sunflower** — damaged yellow sunflower, 8–8.5/10. Decay and structure; dark-horse entry.

## Individual image notes

### 01. The Last Sunflower
- Subject: aging sunflower with ragged yellow petals and textured seed head.
- Strength: visual tension between beauty and deterioration; soft unobtrusive background.
- Edit: gently reduce yellow highlights; selectively increase seed-head texture; darken bright upper green sepals; subtle edge burn. Preserve damage.
- Crop: minimal, retain extending petals.
- Print: yes. Fair: dark horse.

### 02. Night Watch
- Subject: close-up orb-weaver in illuminated web against black.
- Strength: warm spider against darkness; web emerges in fragments.
- Edit: reduce flash-hot abdomen highlights; deepen distracting blacks without eliminating fine silk; avoid excessive sharpening.
- Crop: retain web geometry.
- Print: definite. Fair: possible.

### 03. After the Bloom
- Subject: nearly spent sunflower, mostly exposed brown center and green sepals.
- Strength: unusual depiction of late-stage decay.
- Weakness: bright sepals and orange background spot dominate; shallow depth of field.
- Edit: reduce green luminance/highlights; tame orange blur; emphasize center selectively without fabricating sharpness.
- Crop: test modest right/top trim.
- Print: lower priority, ~6.5–7/10. Fair: no.

### 04. Fractured Gold
- Subject: dense, weathered yellow marigold-like flower.
- Strength: folded petals frame a dark center, strong dark backdrop.
- Weakness: harsh direct light compresses yellow detail.
- Edit: reduce highlights/yellow luminance, selectively emphasize central texture and brown edges.
- Crop: retain scale and partly clipped lower petals.
- Print: possible, ~8/10. Fair: lower priority.

### 05. Architecture of Silk
- Subject: broad orb web with tiny spider near center.
- Strength: monumental geometric structure, interrupted symmetry, immediate impact and close-up discovery.
- Edit: darken brown vertical element on left and peripheral distractions, preserve fine strands, moderate bright spider.
- Crop: avoid forcing symmetry.
- Print: definite, consider large after resolution check. Fair: top three.

### 06. Close Encounter
- Subject: square close-up portrait of a young child.
- Strength: immediate eye contact, intimacy and spontaneous character.
- Weakness: softness, bright forehead and pronounced color cast.
- Edit: gently control face highlights, modest cyan correction, preserve nostalgic softness; no aggressive sharpening.
- Crop: keep square.
- Print: yes as personal photograph; competition suitability not established.

### 07. Climbing Stripes
- Subject: first black swallowtail caterpillar on wet green foliage.
- Strength: black/green/yellow contrast, readable body, upward diagonal into stem, water droplets.
- Edit: tame bright upper-right blur and luminous leaves, protect body detail.
- Crop: minimal; retain direction and negative space.
- Print: yes, ~8.5–9/10. Fair: possible.

### 08. Among the Raindrops
- Subject: second, tighter caterpillar composition.
- Strength: particularly attractive water droplets.
- Weakness: foreground leaves obscure body and break its line.
- Edit: moderate leaf brightness.
- Selection: choose Climbing Stripes instead; ~8/10.

### 09. Orange Against Blue — centered version
- Subject: orange marigold on blue-green garden background, partial flower upper right.
- Strength: petal texture, warm/cool separation.
- Edit: subtle interior shadow depth; moderate cyan saturation if necessary.
- Selection: good print (~8.5/10), but prefer off-center version.

### 10. Orange Against Blue — off-center version
- Subject: marigold left of center with secondary bloom upper right.
- Strength: breathing room, diagonal balance, rich petal structure.
- Edit: experiment with restrained cyan reduction; selective center texture.
- Crop: keep upper-right secondary flower.
- Print: yes, ~9/10. Fair: possible.

### 11. Garden Visitor — first profile
- Subject: dark bee on yellow marigold.
- Strength: clear profile and leg contact.
- Weakness: distracting background blooms.
- Edit: lift bee shadows selectively, moderate orange bloom.
- Selection: alternate only, ~8–8.5/10.

### 12. Garden Visitor — wider environmental profile
- Subject: bee and yellow marigold with garden context.
- Strength: diagonal connection among background blooms and main subject.
- Edit: slight bee shadow lift and top orange burn.
- Selection: better than first profile, ~8.5–9/10.

### 13. Garden Visitor — close profile
- Subject: larger dark bee on yellow marigold with orange bloom above.
- Strength: detailed thorax, legs, antennae and clear physical contact; balanced environment.
- Edit: minimal bee shadow recovery, gentle local detail; suppress distracting peripheral yellow/orange.
- Selection: best of the three yellow-marigold bee shots, ~9–9.25/10. Fair: potential alternate.

### 14. Face to Face
- Subject: bee facing lens from within weathered orange flower.
- Strength: strong sense of encounter, separated antennae, asymmetrical petal stage and layered background.
- Edit: restrained head/eye contrast and sharpness, preserve dark abdomen, reduce hot orange highlights.
- Crop: preserve surrounding petals and background.
- Print: definite, ~9.25–9.5/10. Fair: top three.

### 15. Suspended in the Woods
- Subject: small spider in nearly invisible web against sunlit woodland bokeh.
- Strength: atmospheric scale, negative space and delayed discovery.
- Edit: subtle local spider lift and fine-strand contrast, avoid overrevealing the web or overprocessing bokeh.
- Crop: minimal.
- Print: definite, ~9–9.5/10. Fair: top three.

## Pre-submission checklist
- Locate and map actual original filenames to working titles; avoid renaming masters until mapping is confirmed.
- Check RAW resolution, focus at 100%, clipping, noise and print output.
- Produce non-destructive edits and proof prints.
- Check official 2027 Indiana State Fair photography categories, sizes, mounting rules, deadlines and image manipulation rules once published.
- Avoid submitting near-duplicate frames if entry limits or judging strategy argue against them.
- Treat the ratings as editorial preferences, not verified competition outcomes.
